# 2026-10-09 — TensorRT-LLM's memory check counts the engine's own layout of the weights and a 2 GiB runtime

- **Context:** on an RTX 5090 Laptop (Windows, WSL, 23.57 GiB free of 24 GiB) the check passed `nvidia/Qwen3.8-27B-NVFP4` at 22.8 GiB needed, and the load never got ready: twice, 7 and 11 minutes, until the app was restarted. `trtllm-serve` (1.3.0rc29) held 21.71 GiB of weights inside torch for 20.42 GiB of safetensors and 2.12 GiB outside it (0.32 GiB of that other processes'), so weights and runtime alone took the card; under WDDM the driver then spills into shared system memory (1.93 GiB in the Windows counters, the GPU at 100% and 62 W) instead of raising out-of-memory, so nothing the exit classifier reads ever appears and the load waits out its readiness timeout. The engine's loader keeps Qwen3.5's linear-attention `in_proj_qkvz` in bf16 whatever the checkpoint stores (`_add_qkvz_bf16_workaround`, "temporary") and never reads `mtp.*` layers: `NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Lightning-30B-A3B-NVFP4` held 18.23 GiB for 20.08 GiB of files (2.49 GiB of MTP unread) and 1.92 GiB outside torch, and loaded at a 21.47 GiB peak. The check counted file bytes and a 1.5 GiB runtime measured on an RTX 4070 Laptop.
- **Decision:** `tensorrtLlmMemoryNeed` adds `weightLayoutAllowanceBytes` — 5% of the weight bytes (`TENSORRT_LLM_WEIGHT_LAYOUT_ALLOWANCE`), reported as `weight_layout_bytes` — and `TENSORRT_LLM_RUNTIME_OVERHEAD_BYTES` is 2 GiB. 5% is what separates the two measured checkpoints on that card: Qwen3.8-27B now needs 24.3 GiB and is refused, Nemotron needs 23.4 GiB and still passes. Modelling the loader per architecture (MTP bytes, qkvz in bf16) was not taken: MTP bytes are known only once safetensors headers are read, which the pre-download check route never does, and the qkvz arithmetic did not reproduce the measured figure.
- **Consequences:** every TensorRT-LLM checkpoint needs 5% of its weights and 0.5 GiB more than before; checkpoints near a card's edge that the engine stores at file size (dense FP8 Llama) are refused sooner — a 75 GB FP8 70B-class checkpoint at 8k context no longer fits an 80 GiB H100, a 72 GB one does. Nemotron's pass on the 5090 Laptop has 0.17 GiB to spare because its unread MTP bytes are still counted. A load that does spill under WDDM still hangs until its readiness timeout; detecting the spill is a separate change. The allowance is two points of measurement and should be re-measured with each new engine image.
